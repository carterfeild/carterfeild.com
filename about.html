About

About CarterFeild PR

CarterFeild PR was launched in 2025. It is a modern public relations and advertising agency dedicated to helping brands stand out, connect authentically, and grow with purpose. We specialize in strategic communication, social media management, event coverage, and compelling content creation that captures attention and builds lasting impressions. Whether you're a startup or an established brand, CarterFeild PR is here to elevate your story.

Who is Carter Feild?

Carter Feild is the founder and operator of CarterFeild PR. Carter graduated from High Point University in May 2026 with a Bachelor of Arts in Advertising, Public Relations & Strategic Communication. She is continuing her studies at High Point University's Communication and Business Leadership Masters Program to enhance her skills in compelling storytelling and engaging advertising.

With a broad background in strategic communications, advertising, public relations and a love for helping people and brands shine, Carter launched CarterFeild PR to bring fresh, authentic, and impactful PR ideas to life. Carter has a growing passion for creativity and innovation, allowing her to thrive in making meaningful connections and lasting impressions. When you work with Carter, you are not just getting a service — you're getting a partner who's all in.
